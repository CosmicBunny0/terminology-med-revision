Foundations L1 · L2

Lecture 1 — Introduction: definition, word parts, structural analysis

Medical terminology — definition L1 p9

  • It is the language used to precisely describe the human body including its components, processes, conditions affecting it, and procedures performed upon it.
  • Medical terminology is used in the field of medicine and pharmacy.

لغة بتوصف جسم الإنسان بدقة: مكوناته، العمليات اللي بتحصل فيه، الحالات اللي بتأثر عليه، والإجراءات اللي بتتعمل عليه.

Word parts & origin L1 p10

  • Most medical terms are a combination of word parts: prefix, root (stem), suffix.
  • Most of them come from Latin or Greek words.
  • Other languages such as English, German and French.

معظم الكلمات Latin أو Greek.

The word parts L1 p11–12

  • Root: the word part that is the core of the word.
  • Prefix: a word part attached to the beginning of a word root to modify its meaning.
  • Suffix: a word part attached to the end of the word root to modify its meaning.
  • Combining vowel: a word part, usually an “o” or “i”.

الـ Root هو قلب الكلمة. الـ prefix في الأول والـ suffix في الآخر، والاتنين بيعدّلوا المعنى.

Defining medical terms through word structural analysis L1 p13, 16–20

  • You can define a term according to word structure analysis by: prefix, root, suffix.
  • Patterns on the slides: Root + suffix (lipemia) · Prefix + root + suffix (hyperlipemia) · Root + combining vowel + suffix (carcinogen).

عشان تعرّف أي term: قسّمه prefix / root / suffix وهات معنى كل جزء.

Competency (intro slide) L1 p6–7

  • It is a set of characteristics including knowledge, skills and attitude. All are integrated within a situation which takes place in a setting.
  • Consists of a description of the essential skills, knowledge, attitudes, and behaviors required for effective performance of a real-world task or activity (Mrowicki, 1986).
  • الجدارات = Knowledge + Skills + Behaviors + Attitudes · الكفاءات = القدرة على توظيف هذه العناصر في الأداء الفعلي

Lecture 2 — Medical term components: combining vowels, rules, pronunciation, plurals

Combining vowels L2 p4–8

  • [o] is the most common combining vowel; [i] is the second most common.
  • When a medical term has more than one root, each is joined by a vowel.
  • They are used to join: (i) root to root, (ii) root to suffix.
  • Why? To ease pronunciation.
  • Is their presence mandatory? No — not all medical terms have combining vowels.
  • Additional roots: often a medical term is formed of two or more roots (hyper/lip/o/protein/emia).

o الأشهر وبعدها i. بتربط root بـ root أو root بـ suffix، ومش إجبارية.

Formation of medical terms L2 p17

  • All medical terms build from the root.
  • Prefixes and suffixes are attached to the root to modify its meaning.
  • Often two or more roots are linked before being modified.

Rules for building terms L2 p9

Memorizing and spelling basic medical word components are the first steps for learning how to form medical terms. The next step is to construct the words using the following steps (rules).

#RuleExample
1A combining vowel is used to join root to root as well as to any suffix beginning with a consonant.

o بين root وroot، وقبل أي suffix بيبدأ بـ consonant.

electr/o/cardi/o/gram — electrical record of the heart L2 p10
2A combining vowel is not used before a suffix that begins with a vowel.

الـ suffix بيبدأ بـ vowel؟ يبقى مفيش o.

vas + ectomy → vasectomy — excision of a vessel L2 p12–13
3If the root ends in a vowel and the suffix begins with the same vowel, drop the final vowel from the root and do not use a combining vowel.

نفس الحرف مرتين؟ شيل حرف الـ root.

cardi + itis → carditis — inflammation of heart L2 p14
4When a prefix ends in a vowel and the root begins with a vowel, the final vowel is dropped from the prefix.

هنا اللي بيتشال حرف الـ prefix مش الـ root.

para + enter + al → parenteral — pertaining to alongside of the intestine L2 p15
4bMost often a combining vowel is inserted between two roots even when the second root begins with a vowel.

بين two roots حط o حتى لو التاني بيبدأ بـ vowel.

cardi/o/esophag/eal — pertaining to heart and esophagus L2 p16

Lecture 4 — Prefixes (time/position, disease, degree, negative) + suffixes (specialties, condition, disease)

Color code for medical terminology L4 p2

  • Prefix- (blue): meaning before the root · modifies the meaning · e.g. hyper-, hypo-, anti-, pre-
  • -Root- (green): main meaning of the term · usually indicates the organ, system or action · e.g. cardi-, nephro-, hepat-, gastr-
  • -Suffix (pink): meaning after the root · indicates condition, procedure, disease, drug class, etc. · e.g. -itis, -emia, -ology, -logy, -ase

Prefix categories (L4) L4 p3

  • Time and position
  • Disease
  • Degree
  • Negative prefix

Suffixes L4 p9

  • Suffixes are the ending part of medical terms.
  • Suffixes tend to bring an action to the medical terms.
  • Medical suffixes can be categorized into: specialties, condition of, disease, small, surgery, adjective suffix.

الـ suffix آخر الكلمة وبيدّيها action.

Disease — definition L4 p12

  • A condition of a person, animal, or plant in which its body or structure is harmed because an organ or part is unable to work as it usually does.
